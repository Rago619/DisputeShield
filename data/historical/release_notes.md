# TestPay Product Release Notes (Months 1–22)

### Month 4 (February 2025)
* **Photo Attachments**: Teams can now attach before-and-after job photos via the technician mobile app.
* **Auto-Receipts**: Introduced automatic SMS/Email receipts when customer contact info is present.

### Month 8 (June 2025)
* **Technician GPS Check-In**: Added automatic geotagging and technician timestamp upon arrival at customer premises.
* **Completion Signature**: Added customer digital sign-off on invoice completion.

### Month 12 (October 2025)
* **Card on File & Keyed-In Processing**: Teams can now manually key in card numbers for over-the-phone remote payments.
* **AVS & CVV Checks**: Keyed-in transactions now record Address Verification Service (AVS) and Card Verification Value (CVV) response codes.

### Month 17 (March 2026)
* **Signed Estimates**: Digital estimate approvals with customer acceptance before job work begins.
* **Dispute Center v1**: Basic portal allowing merchants to view incoming chargebacks and submit documentary evidence.
