# TestPay release notes, November 2024 to August 2026

**November 2024.** Dispute records start. A dispute is recorded on the date the card network notifies us.

**November 2024.** Technicians can attach before and after photos to a job from the mobile app. Photos can be added to an invoice at any time, including after the job is closed.

**December 2025.** Completion signature: the customer can sign on the technician's phone when the job is finished. Rolled out team by team over the following three months. Each team chooses whether to use it.

**June 2026.** Invoices show a status: open, paid or disputed. The status was backfilled for older invoices.
